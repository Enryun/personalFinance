Applies to the homepage and contact page. App marketing pages retain their own identity.

Skill guidance: UI/UX Pro Max Portfolio/Personal product match; Editorial Grid / Magazine and Exaggerated Minimalism style matches; serif/sans typography guidance. Use recommendations selectively for a professional developer portfolio, rather than the generic generator's enterprise palette.
